# Plan — Jan Vidovič, portfolio

Vertical slices. Each task is one commit and ends in something that renders.

## Task 1 — Token system and shell

**Acceptance criteria**
- `src/index.css` defines the literal scale, the semantic layer, type, space,
  radius, elevation and motion for dark and light, with no raw hex in a
  component.
- Every foreground/background pairing meets WCAG AA.
- The app mounts with the rail, the page frame and a theme toggle.
- `npm run lint` and `npm run build` pass.

## Task 2 — Hero and the monogram plate

**Acceptance criteria**
- The name is the only level-one heading.
- The positioning line and introduction are real copy, not placeholders.
- Two calls to action, in-page links.
- The plate shows the factual readout: based, studying, working, languages.
- The plate has crop-marks and the monogram set in the display face.

## Task 3 — Profile section

**Acceptance criteria**
- Two paragraphs of real copy about who he is.
- Three entries on what he likes to work on, each tied to a named project.
- A closing line on why he moves between stacks.

## Task 4 — Work section

**Acceptance criteria**
- All four projects render: name, year, kind, body, stack, links.
- SmartStudy links to its live site and its public source.
- The three private projects are marked "Source not published" rather than
  showing a broken link.
- No placeholder or lorem text anywhere.

## Task 5 — Stack section, the calibration chart

**Acceptance criteria**
- Two groups: authored and generated by the toolchain.
- Each bar is proportional to its real byte count, scaled within its group.
- Every row names the project that proves it.
- The generated group is visually de-emphasised and carries the note
  explaining why it is separate.
- A spec sheet of tools and infrastructure below the chart.

## Task 6 — Path and contact sections

**Acceptance criteria**
- Three jobs and one programme of study, each with a period and a place.
- The closing line about non-software work.
- Email, phone, GitHub and location, with working `mailto:` and `tel:` links.
- A download link to the CV PDF, which exists in `public/`.

## Task 7 — Routing, legal pages and deployment setup

**Acceptance criteria**
- `/privacy`, `/imprint` and `/accessibility` render, each with its own title
  and description.
- An unknown path renders a 404 page rather than a blank screen.
- The base path comes from `BASE_PATH`, normalised to a trailing slash.
- The GitHub Pages 404 fallback and the router basename are in place.
- `vercel.json`, the Pages workflow, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and
  `.nojekyll` exist.
- A deep link to `/privacy` resolves on both hosts.

## Task 8 — Tests

**Acceptance criteria**
- `formatBytes` and `barWidth` are unit-tested, including the empty-set case.
- `restorePath` is tested for the no-stash, restore, clear-once and
  query-and-hash cases.
- The Hero renders the name, the calls to action and the positioning line.
- Layout and CSS are not unit-tested.

## Task 9 — Browser verification and critique

**Acceptance criteria**
- No console errors and no failed requests at 1440px, 768px and 390px.
- The page renders the intended content, not a blank shell.
- Navigation and the theme toggle work.
- A critique against the nine-axis rubric has no Blocking or Required findings
  outstanding.

## Task 10 — Performance, SEO and legal

**Acceptance criteria**
- Lighthouse passes on desktop and mobile, with no accessibility failures.
- Unique title and description per route, canonical URLs, Open Graph tags.
- A real favicon, `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` with real URLs.
- The legal pages name the right things and assert nothing that has not been
  reviewed.
